# Draft requests for existing records — not submitted

These requests are prepared for the user to review and submit. No agency or
provider has been contacted on the user's behalf.

## Common requested fields

Please provide existing machine-readable equipment inventory records for the
specified corridor, including, where maintained:

- Stable site and individual equipment/asset IDs; site-to-device relationships.
- Latitude/longitude, coordinate system/datum, stated positional accuracy and
  whether each coordinate represents the actual equipment mount, a road point,
  intersection, monitoring site or coverage-zone reference.
- Equipment purpose: traffic monitoring, automated speed/red-light enforcement,
  plate reading/toll collection, radar/speed display or other sensor.
- Road, carriageway, direction and lane association, where maintained.
- Installation, relocation, retirement and last physical verification dates.
- Current inventory status, existing maintenance/replacement history, and any
  publicly releasable asset-location photographs or as-built plans.
- For mobile enforcement, existing deployment-location records with their
  observation/deployment times and valid periods. If historical data is released,
  please identify its age; it will not be represented as a live position.
- Existing published speed-rule schedules and source dates, where maintained;
  image/data redistribution terms and an existing public feed if available.

This request seeks existing records, not creation of new surveys or reports.
Please identify unavailable/withheld fields and any publicly accessible source
or applicable access/licensing process. Device images need not include plates,
faces or other identifying information.

## New York scope

Potential record custodians: NYC DOT, NYSDOT and the Port Authority of New York
and New Jersey, according to the owner of the equipment.

Corridor: central Harlem approach streets, Harlem River Drive, the I-95 /
Trans-Manhattan connection, and George Washington Bridge approaches.
Please distinguish traffic-monitoring cameras from citation cameras. NYC DOT
camera-site UUIDs are included in the inventory for reconciliation.

## New Jersey scope

Potential custodians: NJDOT, NJ Turnpike Authority and Port Authority, according
to equipment ownership.

Corridor: George Washington Bridge/Fort Lee–Teaneck I-95 connection and I-80
west through Bergen, Passaic, Morris and Warren counties to the Delaware crossing.
Please supply the existing public/developer access process for the 511NJ camera
catalog, in addition to any existing device-level point layer.

## Pennsylvania scope

enforcement program administrator; direct records requests to the actual holder.

Corridor: I-80 from the Delaware crossing to Exit 161, US-220/PA-26 connections,
and I-99/US-220 south to Tyrone. The existing project inventory is insufficient
to establish current mobile enforcement-device coordinates.
